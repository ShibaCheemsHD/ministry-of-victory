## Direction contract

THESIS: The Ministry of Victory is Super Earth's decision desk: it calls the Major Order the way election night calls a race. Each objective is a race with a projection against the 100% line, called only when the numbers settle it, and the one move that changes the outcome is announced first. It refuses the sci-fi HUD terminal (the old site) and the generic SaaS card grid.

OWN-WORLD (pinned by the user): the game's war-table interface. Charcoal panels with cut corners (top-left, bottom-right) over deep space with a faint star field and scanlines; Super Earth yellow for the call band, actions and selection; hazard-striped rails and hatched label strips; segmented progress bars; the call's planet as a slowly turning hologram with its progress as an orbit ring; Q / E bumpers on the tab bar.
