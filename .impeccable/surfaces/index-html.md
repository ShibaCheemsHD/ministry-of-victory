It refuses the sci-fi HUD terminal (the old site) and the generic SaaS card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Night studio: ink-navy ground with a lighter navy desk layer, white type, Super Earth yellow as the network band (masthead, the call's banner, the CALLED stamp) with black type on it. Faction colours only as small planet swatches. Big Shoulders Display for calls and race names, Public Sans for everything else with tabular figures. Hairline rules, square corners, dense rows.
